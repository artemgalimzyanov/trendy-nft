One picture a day of what the world is talking about.

We are surrounded by news and endless noise. Scrolling through it often leaves us anxious, sad or angry, and checking it again and again can become a habit that is hard to break.

Trendy is a calmer way to keep up. Every day it reads headlines from about 20 newspapers around the globe, picks the stories most of them are covering, and turns them into a single AI-drawn-style cartoon.

Each picture is kept forever on decentralized storage, and day by day they grow into a calendar of world news.
